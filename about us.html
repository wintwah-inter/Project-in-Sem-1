CV's About Us

Our Story

Founded in 2024 by three like-mined friends- Kai, Aylia, Chal. We founded Traditional Costumes out of a shared love for our heritage. Our journey is simple: we want to blend traditional craftsmanship with modern style, making it posssible for everyone to wear our culture pride.

Our Vision

Traditional Costume aims to revive disappearing traditional costumes and showcase it to the world through modern and innovtive designs.

Why Choose Us

  • Authenticity We conduct in-depth research on traditional design to ensure every piece is crafted is meticulous attention to its original heritages.
  • Quality First From the selection of fabrics to the final stitch,every detail is thoroughly inspected before reaching our customers.
  • Passion For Heritage More than just a business, we are driven by a profound desire to preserve and protect our cultural legacy.
